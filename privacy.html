Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

This privacy policy explains how information may be handled when you visit this website or use Hermes Agent. This site is a lightweight informational homepage hosted through GitHub Pages. Hermes Agent itself may run locally, on infrastructure you control, or through connected services; the privacy behavior of those environments depends on the configuration and providers you choose.

Information this site receives

This site does not provide a user account, contact form, analytics dashboard, advertising network, or hosted assistant service. GitHub Pages and GitHub may process ordinary technical information needed to deliver the site, such as request metadata, browser information, and approximate network information, under GitHub's own policies and infrastructure.

Information handled by Hermes

When Hermes is configured to work with files, email, calendars, cloud services, messaging platforms, or other connectors, it may process the content required to complete your instructions. That can include prompts, documents, source material, metadata, and results returned by connected providers. Hermes should be configured with least-privilege access, and you remain responsible for deciding which accounts and data sources to connect.

Third-party services

Hermes can use model providers, GitHub, Google Workspace, Obsidian vaults, messaging platforms, and other services selected by the operator. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms. Data sent to a third party is governed by the operator's configuration and that provider's practices; this site does not control or independently audit those providers.

Data retention and security

Retention depends on the deployment, provider, logs, token storage, and local configuration. Do not provide credentials, payment details, secrets, or sensitive personal information unless the relevant workflow is designed and authorized to handle it. Use revocable credentials and review stored tokens, logs, and connected-app permissions regularly.

Your choices

You can stop using the site, revoke connected-app access through the relevant provider, remove local data, or disable a Hermes integration. For questions about this site or its repository, open an issue or discussion in the GitHub profile repository.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the site or its connected workflows change. The revision date above indicates the latest update.
