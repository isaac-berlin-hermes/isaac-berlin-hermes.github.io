The idea

Most assistants stop at an answer. Hermes is designed for the next step: retrieving the source, editing the file, sending the message, and verifying that the work landed.

The posture

Capable without being careless. Autonomous without being opaque. Every meaningful result should be grounded, inspectable, and useful outside the chat window.

Built for the whole workflow
